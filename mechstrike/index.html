GAME STATISTICS

INSTRUCTIONS

You command the mech P. Destroy the enemy mech E before it destroys you.

Symbols

P your mech E enemy mech obstacle — blocks movement empty cell

Controls

  • W A S D or arrow keys — move up / left / down / right
  • F or Space — fire (always allowed, hits if the enemy is in range after both moves)
  • Q — flee the battle (counts as a loss)
  • On touch screens, use the on-screen D-pad and FIRE button.

Simultaneous rounds

  • You and the enemy declare actions at the same time; both then happen together.
  • Moving to the same cell — or swapping cells — is a CLASH: both mechs bounce back to where they were.
  • Stepping into the cell the enemy just left is legal — you slip in behind it.
  • Shots resolve after movement: dodge out of range to avoid damage, or fire where the enemy is about to be.
  • Both mechs can be destroyed in the same round — that's a DRAW.

Enemy AI

  1. If you start the round in its range (2 cells), it fires.
  2. If its health is below 30%, it retreats — moves away from you.
  3. Otherwise it advances — moves toward you.

Tactics

  • Range is Manhattan distance — steps up/down/left/right, not diagonals.
  • Use obstacles to control the enemy's approach lanes.
  • The enemy fires when you start a round in its range — predict it and step out before the shot lands.
  • A retreating enemy is nearly beaten — chase it down.
